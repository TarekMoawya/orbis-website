Grantira

Law office management for firms in Egypt. Grantira keeps cases, clients and powers of attorney in one place. Lawyers see today's hearings, rulings still to record and papers out with process servers, and the court agenda shows every session by lawyer and by month.

  • Cases, clients and powers of attorney
  • Court agenda with Egyptian public holidays
  • Paper and cash custody tracking
  • Finance, team and multiple branches
  • Full Arabic interface, light and dark modes
HealthcareArabic & EnglishDesktop app

Orbis Clinic Suite

Dental clinic management from reception to invoice. Reception books and checks in patients, the doctor sees who is waiting, charts each tooth and records the plan, and invoices pull the treatment and supplies used straight from stock.

  • Patient files with history, X-rays and photos
  • Interactive dental chart, adult and child teeth
  • Appointments by doctor, treatment and duration
  • Invoices linked to inventory
  • Switches between Arabic and English
